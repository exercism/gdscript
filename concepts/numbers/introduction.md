# Introduction

GDScript has 4 different types of built-in numbers: integers ([`int`][int]), floating-point ([`float`][float]), 2D Vectors ([`Vector2`][Vector2]) and 3D Vectors ([`Vector3`][Vector3]).

Hexadecimal (hex), octal (oct) and binary (bin) numbers are identified as `str`.

GDScript fully supports arithmetic between these different number types(?), and will convert narrower numbers to match their less narrow counterparts when used with the binary arithmetic operators (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `//`, and `%`).
